Subscriptions

QuoteSync Premium subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple ID subscription settings.

Use Restore in QuoteSync with the App Store account that purchased the subscription. Your iCloud account is separate from your App Store purchase account. The Watch companion refreshes premium access through its paired iPhone.

Version 2.0: iCloud Sync

On each participating iPhone, iPad or Mac, open Settings in QuoteSync, then Subscription & Sync. Enable iCloud Sync using the same iCloud account on each device. Open the apps and use Sync now to check the latest result. Version 1.0 does not include this feature.

If changes are delayed, check the displayed sync status, internet connection and available iCloud storage. Review any reported conflict before relying on another device's copy. Sync is not an independent backup; retain important PDF exports separately.

Version 2.0: Apple Watch

Open QuoteSync on the paired iPhone, verify premium access, then use Refresh on Watch. Follow-ups and recent activity come from the iPhone. Complete and Snooze actions are not finished until acknowledged by the phone. Notification permission is optional.

Removing Workspace Data

Turning off sync does not erase existing cloud or device copies. Pause sync on every participating device before removing cloud data. Revisions, offline devices, Watch caches and shared exports may retain copies. See the Privacy Policy and contact support for assistance; do not send confidential client documents or account passwords.
