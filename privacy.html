Privacy Policy

Updated: November 12, 2026. Covers QuoteSync 1.0 and 2.0, including TestFlight versions.

QuoteSync is built to keep proposal work private and local by default.

Information Stored in the App

QuoteSync may store the following information on your device:

  • Proposal titles, client names, scope, pricing, status, and timeline details.
  • Project milestones, deliverables, payment states, and notes.
  • Brand kit settings, including company name, initials, accent color, typography, and an optional logo selected by you.
  • Reusable content library entries.
  • Subscription access state and selected plan.

This information is stored locally on your device. QuoteSync does not currently operate a developer server and does not upload proposal data to the developer.

Optional iCloud Sync in Version 2.0

Version 1.0 keeps the workspace locally. In version 2.0, iCloud Sync is optional and off by default. If you enable it, QuoteSync transfers your workspace to your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit service. Participating iPhone, iPad and Mac devices must use the same iCloud account and have sync enabled. There is no separate QuoteSync account or developer-hosted workspace server.

The synchronized workspace includes the proposal, client contact, pricing, project, brand image, reusable content, activity and revision information you enter. Workspace and device identifiers help reconcile edits and keep workspaces separate. Only store information you are entitled to use. Sync runs while the app is active; connectivity, account availability and iCloud storage limits can delay it.

Apple Watch in Version 2.0

The companion app receives a limited snapshot from your paired iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity service. This can include proposal titles, client names, values, statuses, follow-up dates and recent activity. The Watch stores its snapshot and action queue locally. Completion and snooze requests remain pending until acknowledged by the paired iPhone. Access may require a refresh from iPhone. Follow-up notification permission is optional.

Purchases

QuoteSync uses Apple’s StoreKit system for subscriptions. Subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations, and billing are handled by Apple under your Apple ID and Apple’s terms.

Photos

If you choose to add a logo, QuoteSync uses Apple’s photo picker so you can select an image. The selected image is used only for your brand kit and generated proposal materials inside the app.

Tracking and Advertising

QuoteSync does not track users across apps or websites. QuoteSync does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics.

Sharing and Export

QuoteSync can generate proposal PDFs and open system sharing tools. You choose where exported files are sent. Sharing destinations are handled by your operating system and the apps you select.

Data Deletion

You can edit or remove workspace content through the app's available controls. Turning off iCloud Sync stops synchronization on that device; it does not delete data already in iCloud or on other devices. Removing the app from one device does not erase cloud copies, other devices' copies, backups, exports or files shared with recipients. Removing a Mac app may leave its local application data behind.

Revision history, conflict copies and deletion markers may be retained to reconcile changes. Removing an item from the interface does not guarantee immediate erasure from every revision or offline device. Pause sync on all participating devices before removing cloud data to prevent a retained local copy from uploading again. Contact support for help with data removal. The developer cannot erase your private workspace through a QuoteSync account service, and there is currently no in-app Delete Cloud Workspace command.

Changes

This policy will be updated when QuoteSync's data practices change. The optional sync and Watch sections apply to version 2.0 and later versions that provide those features, not to the local-only version 1.0.
